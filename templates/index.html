💡 How to Run a 30-Second Demo

1
Generate Payment: Go to the Payer Dashboard, enter an amount (e.g. ₹50.00), and click Generate & Sign. Then click Copy Payment JSON.
2
Verify Offline: Go to the Merchant POS, paste the copied JSON, and click Verify Payment. It verifies instantly offline and queues for sync.
3
Sync with Bank: On the Merchant POS, click Sync with Bank. The payment is permanently settled into the Bank ledger.
